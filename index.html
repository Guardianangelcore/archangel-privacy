🛡️ Archangel OS – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026  |  Developer: GA Labs (Guardian Angel)

1. Introduction

Archangel OS ("the App") is a personal health, safety, and survival management platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with the highest level of security and transparency.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, password (hashed), account preferences
  • Health data: metrics you manually enter (e.g. blood pressure, weight, medications)
  • Emergency contacts: names and phone numbers you provide
  • Location data: only when you explicitly trigger SOS – never in background
  • Device data: device type, OS version, app version (for crash reporting only)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the App's core functionality
  • To send SOS alerts to your designated emergency contacts
  • To personalise your health dashboard and survival preparation checklists
  • To diagnose technical issues and maintain security

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared only with:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and real-time database hosting
  • RevenueCat: subscription and in-app purchase management
  • Law enforcement: only when required by law

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently erased within 30 days.

6. Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Health data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. We apply the principle of minimum necessary data access.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / EU)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to: access your data, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@archangelos.app
